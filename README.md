# Marketing-Campaign-Performance-Analysis-Using-Microsoft-Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing campaign data using Microsoft Excel to evaluate campaign effectiveness, advertising expenditure, revenue generation, and profitability.

The analysis uses Pivot Tables, key performance indicators (KPIs), and dashboard visualizations to identify trends and generate actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze marketing campaign performance across different channels.
* Compare advertising spend and revenue.
* Evaluate campaign effectiveness using marketing KPIs.
* Identify monthly and regional performance trends.
* Identify campaigns that require further investigation.
* Provide data-driven recommendations for marketing optimization.

## 🛠️ Tools and Techniques

* Microsoft Excel
* Data exploration and quality checks
* Pivot Tables
* KPI analysis
* Data visualization
* Dashboard reporting

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 500 campaign records and 18 columns, covering December 2024 to June 2025.

Key fields include:

* Campaign ID and Date
* Marketing Channel and Region
* Budget and Advertising Spend
* Impressions and Clicks
* Conversions and Revenue

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

* Click-Through Rate (CTR)
* Conversion Rate
* Return on Advertising Spend (ROAS)
* Cost Per Click (CPC)
* Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)
* Profit after Advertising Spend
* Profit Margin

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Instagram generated the highest revenue, at approximately $1.41 million.
* April 2025 recorded the highest monthly revenue, at approximately $1.23 million.
* The West region generated the highest revenue, at approximately $1.75 million.
* Overall ROAS was approximately 2.09, meaning every $1 spent on advertising generated around $2.09 in revenue.
* 158 out of 500 campaign records generated less revenue than their advertising spend, highlighting areas for further investigation.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Investigate opportunities to optimize advertising budgets across marketing channels.
* Review campaigns with ROAS below 1.
* Compare monthly results while considering differences in campaign volume.
* Investigate regional performance differences.
* Monitor key marketing KPIs regularly to support better decisions.
